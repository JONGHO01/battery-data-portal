# EIS-only DRT analysis

## Corrected noisy L-knee and diagnostics (v0.3.13 / rc-drt-4)

This is the current numerical contract and explicitly supersedes the incorrect clockwise-corner claim in v0.3.12. With x=log(residual), y=log(roughness), t=log(lambda), use the ordinary signed curvature `(x' y'' - y' x'') / (x'^2 + y'^2)^(3/2)`. Hansen's typical noisy L-knee has positive curvature; the concave noise-free scalar shrinkage arc is not that knee. Keep interior, local-maximum, monotonicity, meaningful-tangent and 5% residual guards. Unclear or high-error cases still do not become automatic successes. Known-truth noisy inverse and two-CPE regressions replace the old scalar-success assumption. This corrects direction, not the fundamental limits of L-curve selection or guarantees of an optimal lambda.

The unchanged density is detected with a default **0.2 log10-decade half-prominence width**, converted into sample width for each grid. Explicit `min_width` samples remain a legacy helper override. In-band peak prominence is compared with 5% of the measured-band gamma maximum, so a large extrapolated tail cannot hide a measured-band candidate. Out-of-band candidates retain the global reference, measured-band candidates take priority during close-peak suppression, and the 0.25-decade distance rule remains. All qualifying candidates, original density samples and unchanged ln(tau) regional integrals remain available. The new `width_decades` and `peakDetection` metadata expose these conventions; they are conservative defaults, not universal physical thresholds. Grid-independent detection does not make the inverse solution or resolution independent of M.

`metadata.kkConsistency` is a separate **numerical consistency diagnostic**, not the DRT reconstruction error and not physical pass/fail. The independent `backend/analysis/eis_kk.py` implementation uses a signed-RC measurement model with R0, series-L and inverse-series-C nuisance columns, fixed measured-band log times, modulus weights and scaled SVD least squares. No package installation, normal-equation inverse, source alteration or gamma smoothing is involved. Signed coefficients in this diagnostic do not change the nonnegative DRT model or identify chemical processes.

For at least eight distinct positive frequencies spanning one decade, evaluate orders 3 to min(30, N-3). Mu=1-negative-R-mass/positive-R-mass follows the Lin-KK model-order heuristic. The first candidate with mu<=0.85 and residual<=5% is a **heuristic order selection only**. Unlike the unguarded early-stop algorithm, all bounded orders are evaluated: a low-order mu crossing can be an underfit even for clean synthetic data. If selection is unresolved, preserve the lowest finite full-rank residual but label it inconclusive. Pure resistance, invalid/duplicate/zero inputs, rank/condition failures and soft five-second budgets are explicit inconclusive cases. Preserve `orderCurve`, the selection rule, order, mu and signed `(measured-model)/abs(measured)` residuals in original acquisition order. Small numeric residuals never set `physicalValidationPerformed`, `linearityValidated` or `stationarityValidated` true. This is not a bit-for-bit reproduction of impedance.py or a statistical acceptance test; measuring linearity/stationarity and quantifying uncertainty still require independent experimental evidence.

Both screen/SVG and the compact status expose KK residuals and unresolved order selection separately from `physical validation incomplete`. Old histories without KK say so. Health advertises `eisDrtAnalysisVersion=rc-drt-4`, and EIS calculations on an older backend are blocked until an approved restart. Saved rc-drt-3 results remain history with a recalculation notice; no database migration or automatic replacement is performed. Existing source/provenance/assignment/export contracts are otherwise preserved.

Primary references: [Hansen, Eq.14 and noisy L-curve criterion](https://www.imm.dtu.dk/documents/ftp/tr99/tr15_99.pdf), [Schönleber et al. Lin-KK method](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.electacta.2014.01.034), [official impedance.py validation formulas and heuristic limits](https://impedancepy.readthedocs.io/en/latest/validation.html), [SciPy peak widths in samples](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.signal.find_peaks.html).

## Calculation review (v0.3.12 / rc-drt-3)

Historical review: the automatic curvature direction in this release was incorrect and is superseded by v0.3.13 above. The RC kernel, signed imaginary part, joint real/imaginary fit, ln(tau) quadrature, relative weighting and integrated second derivative remain unchanged. No measurement or returned density is smoothed, averaged or discarded to improve a fit.

- The portal retains Hansen's valid objective `||W(Ax-b)||^2 + lambda^2 ||Bx||^2`, implemented as `[WA; lambda B]`. Metadata explicitly records `regularizationConvention=lambda_squared` and `regularizationPenalty=lambda^2`. pyDRTtools instead names the penalty coefficient lambda; its numeric lambda is not directly interchangeable, especially with different weighting/bases. Existing manual lambda values retain their meaning.
- This release mistakenly treated a concave clockwise scalar arc as the normal noisy L-knee. Do not use that direction as a validity criterion; v0.3.13 corrects it and replaces the regression fixture. The retained guards and original-order diagnostics are numerical heuristics, not universal physical criteria.
- A corner with relative complex RMSE over 5% is not automatically accepted. If even the smallest candidate residual exceeds that numerical guard, `autoSelection.reason=poor_model_fit`; otherwise no defensible corner yields `unclear_corner`. Fallback/manual results are explicitly not automatic success. Small RMSE does not prove linearity, stationarity, Kramers-Kronig consistency or a physical mechanism; no such validation was performed.
- BVLS can return machine-roundoff negatives at a nominal zero bound. Only values within `128 * machine_epsilon * max(1, max(abs(x)))` of zero are projected, recorded in `solverRoundoffCorrection`. Material negative solutions fail rather than being silently clipped. This does not smooth gamma.
- All qualifying peaks remain in stored results/exports. Only peak locations within `[1/(2*pi*f_max), 1/(2*pi*f_min)]` qualify for the five major interpretation-candidate slots. `interpretationEligible=false` peaks stay in the expandable list, without major plot labels. `regionOutsideMeasuredBand=true` marks an integral whose unchanged local-minima boundaries extend beyond that information band; an asterisk/caution does not silently truncate its resistance. All assignments still default to Unassigned and only explicit user choices add a physical name.
- High-error results visibly say interpretation caution, not simply trustworthy completion. Result and API provenance both record rc-drt-3. Old saved results remain history with a recalculation notice; no migration or automatic rewrite is performed.

Primary references checked: [Hansen, The L-curve and its use in inverse problems](https://www.imm.dtu.dk/documents/ftp/tr99/tr15_99.pdf), including the lambda-squared formulation and limitations; [official pyDRTtools regularization code](https://github.com/ciuccislab/pyDRTtools/blob/master/pyDRTtools/parameter_selection.py) and [quadratic formulation](https://github.com/ciuccislab/pyDRTtools/blob/master/pyDRTtools/basics.py); [Hahn et al., optimized DRT process parameters](https://d-nb.info/1207389455/34), which cautions about regularization and discretization dependence; [SciPy bounded least-squares contract](https://docs.scipy.org/doc/scipy/reference/generated/scipy.optimize.lsq_linear.html). The current implementation is not pyDRTtools and does not claim to reproduce its lambda value or a universal optimal result.

## Interpretation candidates and manual assignments (v0.2.8)

HF/MF/LF bands below the DRT X axis are **interpretation candidates, not confirmed resistance identities**. The Korean/English legend describes interfacial/film, charge-transfer and transport/diffusion candidates. Bands cover only the measured time band. Internal boundaries are the one-third and two-thirds quantiles (linear interpolation) of sorted log-time anchors: measured endpoints plus unique in-band peak times. Without peaks this is an equal log-range split. These relative visual boundaries have no universal physical meaning, do not change peak integration intervals, and never assign a mechanism.

The visible peak table has Peak, tau_peak / s, f_peak / Hz, R_region / Ω and Assignment. Defaults are Unassigned. Users select R_SEI, R_ct, R_ion, Diffusion or Other, then press Assignment 저장. Only allowlisted user choices appear beside neutral P labels; Unassigned removes the physical name. R_region remains the gamma integral between neighboring numerical minima, not peak height or a guide-band resistance.

Assignments use `figureEdits.eisDrtAssignments` through the existing permission-checked Dataset PATCH. They are bound to Dataset ID, saved analysis-run ID, spectrum index and peak values. Only the latest explicitly saved assignment set is retained per Dataset; a new calculation starts unassigned. Unknown labels and mismatched contexts are ignored. Failed saves retain a visibly unsaved draft. Current choices appear in SVG/enlargement, peak CSV and metadata JSON; save before leaving to retain them. No raw-data, solver, endpoint, schema, authentication or storage-provider change is required.

## Scope and data flow

The existing EIS renderer dispatches combined and Nyquist views to `public/eis-drt.js`. Standalone Bode uses the prior renderer. The module owns an EIS-only WeakMap and abortable view listeners. It does not change global state, CSS, sidebar, header, login, routing, shared parsers, CYC/CV, or storage configuration.

Existing upload → parser → canonical BDF rows / immutable artifacts → Dataset → EIS adapter → existing authorized `POST /api/datasets/{id}/analyses` (`analysisName=eis-drt`) → `backend/analysis/eis_drt.py` → separate analysis artifact and metadata. GET itself remains read-only; a complete newly opened EIS view can then initiate the guarded automatic POST described above. Existing permissions remain enforced. There is no new endpoint or migration.

SEO/SDE parsers preserve signed imaginary values. Shared BDF mapping does not correct negative-imaginary headers. The EIS adapter normalizes frequency_Hz, z_real_ohm, z_imag_ohm, including original `_raw` headers. Im(Z)/Z'' are preserved; -Im(Z)/-Z'' are negated once. Already corrected canonical values are not negated again. Raw rows are never rewritten. Left coordinates are (Re, −Im); right coordinates are (Re, Im). Input order and sweep breaks are retained, never sorted by real impedance. HF means maximum valid frequency.

## Numerical contract

`Z = Re + i Im`, `omega = 2 pi f`.

`Zfit = Rs + i omega L + integral gamma(ln tau)/(1 + i omega tau) d(ln tau)`.

- M=100 initially; log grid from `0.1/omega_max` to `10/omega_min`.
- Trapezoidal log-grid weights: h internally, h/2 at both endpoints.
- `Z0=median(abs(Z))`, `omega_ref=max(omega)`, epsilon=`1e-12 Z0`.
- Variables r=Rs/Z0, ell=omega_ref L/Z0, g=gamma/Z0. Real kernel q/(1+(omega tau)^2); imaginary kernel −q omega tau/(1+(omega tau)^2).
- Equal real/imaginary weights `Z0/(max(abs(Z),epsilon) sqrt(N))`.
- Gamma-only second differences scaled by `sqrt(h)/h^2`; Rs/L are unregularized.
- Augmented least squares `[WA; lambda B]` with all variables nonnegative; SciPy 1.17.1 `lsq_linear(method="bvls")`, no matrix inverse. Disabling L removes its column and yields exactly zero L.
- Manual lambda starts at 0.001. Optional L-curve uses 41 log-spaced values 1e-6…10. Positive signed-curvature noisy-knee score, interior location, monotonicity, meaningful curve variation/tangents, numerical fit guard and nontrivial RC mass are heuristic reliability checks, not physical validation. Ambiguous corners fall back to the explicitly entered manual lambda with a warning; see rc-drt-4 above.
- Peak P1/P2 regions end at neighboring numerical minima. Region resistance integrates gamma in ln(tau), with interpolated boundaries. Rs is not a peak. Peaks never automatically identify SEI, charge transfer or ionic transport.
- Reconstruction is evaluated at every valid original frequency. `relative_complex_RMSE_percent = 100 sqrt(mean(abs(Zfit-Z)^2 / max(abs(Z),epsilon)^2))`.

## Quality, bounds and persistence

Only missing/nonfinite values and nonpositive frequency are excluded from DRT. Original data remain intact. Duplicate frequencies remain separate and produce a warning; multiple sweeps may not represent one stationary spectrum. Negative imaginary values are valid. Without frequency, both Nyquists remain available and DRT displays its required-input message.

For bounded synchronous work: M=20…200; at most 2,000 source rows; at least three distinct positive frequencies; supported f=1e-12…1e15 Hz and finite impedance magnitude ≤1e100 ohm. Larger inputs are rejected explicitly, not silently averaged/subsampled. Auto-search checks a 45-second budget between solves (not a hard interruption of an individual solve). The browser waits up to 120 seconds; a timeout does not prove server cancellation. Reopen the Fig to check whether a result was saved before retrying.

The measured information band is 1/omega_max…1/omega_min. Yellow outside-band shading warns against overinterpretation. A nonnegative RC model cannot necessarily fit inductive loops or nonstationary data; the serial L term models series inductance only. Error above 5% warns of mismatch, not automatic outlier removal. Defaults are not claimed optimal.

Settings/data/file changes invalidate displayed fits. A revision guard rejects late responses; raw-triplet equality prevents applying saved fits to different data. Saved metadata include lambda, M, tau limits, Rs, L, includeL, sign convention, excluded count, solver, quality, and provenance. CSV exports use numeric fixed columns. Existing SVG/PNG export the same three-panel SVG; metadata JSON includes quality and warnings.
